How Zenda works, screen by screen

Vinuy earns $1,100 a week and wants a house, a car and a trip to Peru. This is what Zenda shows him. Click any screen to try it live with his data.

1 · Discover

Tell us about your money and your goals

Enter your income and your main expenses, then pick your goals: a trip, a car, a home, study or a business. It takes two minutes and needs no bank login. Zenda shows you how much you can save each week.

2 · What's achievable

See which goals you can reach

For each goal, Zenda tells you clearly if it is on track, if it needs a change, or if it needs more time. It also shows the exact amount per week that would make it work.

3 · Prioritise

Choose what matters most

Put your goals in order. Zenda sends your savings to the most important goal first and shows you the date each goal will be reached.

4 · Roadmap

Your plan, step by step

See every goal on a timeline with its date. Move the slider to try a different weekly amount and watch the dates update instantly.

5 · Trade-off

When a goal is too far, see your options

If a goal cannot be reached in time, Zenda gives you clear choices: a smaller amount now, the full amount later, or how much more you would need to save each week.

6 · Progress

One simple check-in every payday

Each payday, Zenda asks: did you save this week? Answer yes, partly, or not this time. You see your streak and how far along you are.

7 · Life changed

Update your numbers any time

If your rent goes up or you get a raise, change the numbers and your plan updates automatically. Nothing you have saved is lost — your progress stays and only the dates move. You see the old dates and the new ones side by side.

8 · Goal reached

Celebrate, then keep going

When you reach a goal, Zenda celebrates with you and automatically moves your weekly savings to the next goal.

9 · Ask Zenda

Ask anything about your plan

A built-in assistant on every screen. Ask why a date lands where it does or what to do this payday — it answers using your own numbers, never guesses, and never sells you anything.

Questions

Can my employer see my numbers?

No. Employer-blindness is enforced in the database, not in the interface: there is no access path from an employer account to any employee's income, goals or progress. The employer sees seat counts and headcount only, and only above a minimum group size.

How does Zenda charge?

Zenda is a workplace benefit: the employer pays a flat fee per seat, per month. Employees pay nothing, ever. We never take a percentage of anyone's savings and never touch anyone's money.

Do I connect my bank?

No bank login. You enter your numbers in two minutes; nothing connects to your accounts and Zenda cannot move money. The check-in each payday is one tap.

Is this financial advice?

No. Zenda gives general information, not personal financial advice. Every projection carries that disclaimer, and Zenda never names products, funds, brokers or coins and never tells you to buy or sell anything.

How does the coach work?

The coach runs on Claude and uses only the numbers Zenda's engine computed for you — it never does its own arithmetic. It can propose a change to a goal; you apply it with one tap, or ignore it. The app works fully without it.

What happens when my situation changes?

Update anything — rent, pay, a new goal — and the roadmap redraws. Nothing you have saved is lost; your progress stays and only the dates move.

What if I leave the company?

Your account, goals and progress are yours. They stay with you when you change jobs.

Who is Zenda for?

Companies first — a benefit for their people. Universities, NGOs and high schools are next.
