What makes Zenda different

Six things we chose on purpose.

1

It starts from the paycheck, not the bank statement

Take-home, essentials, fun — and one goal. The engine is what's left each payday, and everything is built on that number.

2

One engine, honest dates

Integer cents, calendar arithmetic, planning rates shown on screen. The same maths runs on the server and in your browser; change a number and the whole path redraws without losing what you've saved.

3

An AI coach that acts

The Zenda Coach runs on Claude and knows your whole path. Say "Peru after the car" and it shows exactly what moves — then proposes the change. You apply it with one tap. It never does its own arithmetic.

4

Employer-paid, employer-blind

A workplace benefit: flat fee per seat, nothing for employees. Blindness is a database fact, not a promise — the employer's only window is a seat count.

5

Built for life to change

Extra money in, money out for an emergency, a goal moved earlier or later — all first-class, all recalculated, all kept in the record you can see.

6

Three modules, nothing else

Discover → Roadmap → Progress. No feeds to scroll, no products to buy, one question per payday.

Where we're honest about gaps

  • No bank feeds yet. You enter your numbers; automatic feeds are on the roadmap, always optional.
  • No investing, no products — by design. Zenda shows planning rates and never names a fund, broker or coin.
  • Web-first. Installs on a phone as a PWA; native apps come after the workplace rollout.
  • Companies first. Universities, NGOs and high schools are next.

The sentence we build for: "I earn $1,100 a week. Show me the path to Peru, the car and a home — and what changes when my life does."
